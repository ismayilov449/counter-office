Bots

Bots fill the places nobody is playing (8 vs 8 with 6 people = 10 bots) and leave as people join. When every person is dead, the round ends right away — nobody has to watch the bots play.

Party modes

Party modes apply right away (the weapon from the next spawn; classic mode only).

Round times apply from the next round, start money from the next match. Changing the game mode restarts the match.
